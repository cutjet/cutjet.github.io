Privacy Policy

Applies to the Cutjet website and the Cutjet Uploader application. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Cutjet is a short-form video channel. "Cutjet Uploader" is Cutjet's private tool used by the channel owner to publish Cutjet's own videos to Cutjet's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's performance statistics. It is not offered to other users.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

Cutjet Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access and why

When the channel owner connects their Google account, Cutjet Uploader requests permission to:

  • Upload videos to the connected YouTube channel (titles, descriptions, tags and the video file that the owner has approved).
  • Read the channel's information (channel name, subscriber count, list and status of the channel's uploaded videos).
  • Read YouTube Analytics for the channel (views, likes, comments, watch time, audience retention and, where available, revenue estimates) to show the owner how their videos perform.

We do not access any other Google data, and we do not access data of any other YouTube users.

4. How information is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the channel owner's own computer/server. Channel statistics are stored in a local database on the same machine so the owner can see trends and reports. We do not upload this data to any third party.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information obtained through YouTube API Services with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The private Cutjet Uploader dashboard uses a single session cookie only to keep the owner signed in.

7. Retention and deletion

Stored statistics are kept while the channel uses Cutjet Uploader and are deleted within 30 days of a deletion request or of access being revoked. You can revoke Cutjet Uploader's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings; stored tokens then stop working and are deleted.
